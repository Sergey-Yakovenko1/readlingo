Support & FAQ

How do I contact you?

Email support@readlingo.app — we usually reply within two business days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Readlingo, or tap “Manage subscription” in the app's Progress tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged.

I bought Premium on another device. How do I get it back?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the Progress tab and tap “Restore purchases”.

How do refunds work?

Payments are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Do I need an account?

No. Readlingo works without sign-up. Your progress and flashcards are stored on your device.

Who writes the books?

Readlingo Originals are written for learners, and classics are adapted from public-domain works. Texts are drafted with the help of AI and checked by an editorial review step.
