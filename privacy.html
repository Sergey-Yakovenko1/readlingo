Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

The short version. Readlingo has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your reading progress, saved words and settings are stored only on your device. Payments are handled by Apple — we never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Readlingo (“Readlingo”, “we”, “us”) is a language-learning app for iPhone and iPad, developed and published by Serhii Yakovenko. For any privacy question, write to support@readlingo.app.

This policy explains what information the Readlingo app and this website (readlingo.app) handle, and why.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and the app does not send us any personal information. In particular, we do not collect:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details;
  • your location, contacts, photos or microphone input;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics;
  • payment card or bank details.

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software, and we do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, Readlingo saves the following locally on your device:

  • the language you are learning, your level and your daily goal;
  • your reading progress (which chapters you have opened and finished);
  • the words you save as flashcards and their review schedule;
  • reading statistics such as minutes read per day and your streak;
  • reader settings such as font size, colour theme and audio speed.

This data never leaves your device through Readlingo. It may be included in your iCloud or computer backups if you have device backups turned on — those backups are managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Readlingo Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages your subscription. Apple tells the app whether you have an active subscription so it can unlock Premium content; we do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. Apple's handling of this information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Audio and translation features

  • Audio is produced by the text-to-speech voices built into iOS, on your device.
  • Word and sentence translations shipped with each book are part of the book file on your device; looking them up sends nothing anywhere.
  • “Translate with Apple” (optional, for words outside the built-in dictionary) uses Apple's system Translation feature. The text you choose to translate is processed by Apple according to Apple's privacy terms, not by us.

6. Book content

Books are included with the app or downloaded together with app updates. Downloading or reading a book does not tell us who you are or what you read.

7. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and delete support emails when they are no longer needed, normally within 12 months.

8. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering the font files; see Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Children

Readlingo is a general-audience educational app. Because we do not collect personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since the app keeps your learning data only on your device, you can view, change or delete it directly in the app or by deleting the app. For any data we hold because you emailed us, contact support@readlingo.app and we will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

If we add features that change how data is handled — for example optional cloud sync or an account — we will update this policy before releasing them and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

12. Contact

Serhii Yakovenko, developer of Readlingo — support@readlingo.app
